About NestFund Financial

I became a parent at 18. I did not learn credit, saving, or investing at home or at school. I made mistakes that cost years. I also watched the same gaps hit my family. NestFund Financial is my answer to that. It is a practical guide for parents who want to build wealth from the first year of a child's life.

This platform teaches basics, shows the right account to open, and gives you tools to stay consistent. The goal is simple. Make financial literacy normal for families and give every kid a fair shot.
