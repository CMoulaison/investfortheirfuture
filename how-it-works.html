How It Works

Eligibility

  • Child is born on or after January 1, 2025.
  • Parent or legal guardian can open the account.

Funding and growth

  • Government seed of $1,000 when opened.
  • Up to $5,000 per year in family contributions.
  • Growth is tax deferred. Earnings are not taxed each year while they stay in the account.

Approved uses

  • Education or training.
  • First home purchase.
  • Starting a business.
  • Qualified medical expenses above a set threshold.

At age 18

The account can roll into a Traditional IRA in the child's name. That allows the balance to keep compounding for decades. Our calculator will show a projection to age 60.

Penalties

Using funds for non approved purposes can trigger regular income tax on earnings and a 10 percent penalty on the earnings portion.
